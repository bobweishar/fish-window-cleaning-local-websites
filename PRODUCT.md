# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: homeowners in Hinsdale and nearby western/southern Chicago suburbs who want a reliable, careful team for window cleaning and seasonal exterior maintenance.
- Secondary: local storefront, office, restaurant, condominium, association, and property-management buyers who value dependable recurring service.
- Operating user: Dave Weishar's local FISH Window Cleaning franchise team, which needs profitable booked work, route density, and attribution to completed jobs.

## Product Purpose

Create a locally controlled acquisition, proof, and measurement layer for Dave's franchise. The site should help a visitor understand the local team, see credible local work, choose residential or commercial service, and continue into the existing FISH phone or estimate workflow. Success is incremental contribution profit from attributable completed customers in the target cluster.

## Positioning

Dave's local team, backed by FISH standards, already trusted in Hinsdale-area homes and businesses. The site is not a generic lead-generation directory and does not replace the official franchise page; it makes the local operator, work, and service context visible and measurable.

## Operating Context

- The current corporate site and booking flow remain the official franchise destination.
- The new site owns campaign landing experiences, local project proof, analytics, and outbound handoff measurement.
- The immediate operating horizon is a six-to-eight-week fall revenue sprint, followed by a winter build for spring.
- Calls and estimate or booking actions route to the existing business phone and FISH page.
- Residential demand is the initial paid/organic wedge; commercial proof and recurring-route acquisition remain strategically important.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One neutral operator-controlled domain; no town subdomains or thin microsite network.
- Launch around one authoritative Hinsdale residential experience, one commercial proof experience, tracked phone and booking handoffs, and real project stories.
- All useful public organic pages must be crawlable and AI/search-indexable from launch; duplicate paid variants and confirmation/private pages remain noindex.
- GA4, Tag Manager, call and outbound-click events, UTMs, and booked/completed-job reconciliation are required.
- Claims, ratings, customer names, photographs, reviews, pricing, and service availability must be sourced and current. Never invent proof.
- The official FISH logo may be used as a confirmed brand commitment from the user's direction; exact trademark rules remain an operating dependency rather than a design invention.
- The existing FISH booking and phone systems remain authoritative.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve clear recognition of FISH Window Cleaning and Dave's independently owned local franchise.
- Use the official FISH logo and connect visibly to the official franchise page.
- The user does not require the new site to imitate the current corporate website.
- The local-team story should lead; national-scale credibility should support it rather than dominate it.
- Real job photography, uniforms, trucks, local property types, and local people outrank generic stock photography.
- Voice: specific, neighborly, professional, plainspoken, and proof-led; never slick franchise boilerplate or exaggerated luxury language.

## Product Principles

1. Local proof before generic claims.
2. One clear action per page, with measured handoff into the existing FISH workflow.
3. Route density and completed-job economics before raw lead volume.
4. Useful, original service and town content before page count.
5. FISH recognition with local-operator humanity—not corporate-template sameness.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, strong contrast, readable type, descriptive alt text, reduced-motion support, and mobile call/booking paths that remain usable without precision tapping. Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the public site.
